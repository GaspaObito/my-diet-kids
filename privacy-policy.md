# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

## Introduction

My Diet Kids is an educational mobile application designed to help children develop healthy eating habits.

## Information We Collect

The application may collect and store:

- Name
- Age
- Weight
- Height
- Activity level
- Food records
- Water intake records
- Challenge progress
- Walking activity data from the device step counter

## How We Use Information

The collected information is used exclusively to:

- Calculate calorie recommendations
- Calculate water intake goals
- Display progress and achievements
- Track daily challenges
- Improve the user experience

## Data Storage

All information is stored locally on the user's device using SharedPreferences. The application does not sell personal information to third parties.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent user data to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

This application is intended for children under parental supervision. No data is knowingly collected for advertising purposes.
